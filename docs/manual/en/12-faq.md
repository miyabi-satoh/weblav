# Frequently asked questions

## Can I view it from outside my home?

No. WebLAV works only inside the same network.

## Can I show folders on a NAS or another computer?

No. Copy them to the computer running WebLAV.

## Some videos or audio files will not play

Convert them to mp4 (H.264 and AAC) or mp3. They will play on most devices.

## Photos from my iPhone do not have thumbnails

Thumbnails of HEIC files are made by the computer running WebLAV, so they appear only if that computer can open HEIC files. HEIC files open only in browsers that can display HEIC, such as Safari.
In iPhone Settings > Camera > Formats, choose “Most Compatible” so photos you take from now on will appear.

## PDF, Word, and Excel files do not have thumbnails

Thumbnails of videos and documents are made by the computer running WebLAV. Files it cannot make a thumbnail for appear with an icon.
On Windows, PDF files get thumbnails only when a PDF app that makes thumbnails (such as Adobe Acrobat Reader) is installed, and Word and Excel files only when they were saved with a thumbnail. In Word and Excel, check “Save Thumbnail” in the Save As dialog to include one.

## Can I register or manage from a phone?

Yes. Sign in and use it in a browser.
However, you can add shared folders and create the first administrator only on the computer running WebLAV.

## Does every viewer need an account?

No. Anyone can view items with “Anyone” visibility without signing in.
