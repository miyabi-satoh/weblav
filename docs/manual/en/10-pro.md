# Upgrade to Pro

WebLAV is free to use on the Free plan, which limits how many users and items you can register. Pro removes the limits.

## Free limits

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Admins | 1 |
| Editors | 3 |
| Archives | 3 |
| Folders, files, and links | 10 each |

While on Free, see the current counts in the “Pro” section of the “Site settings” tab in the admin pages. Groups have no limit.

## Pro pricing

Pro (personal) is a subscription of 480 yen a month or 4,800 yen a year (tax included). It renews automatically each period.

Pro belongs to an account, and one account can make up to 3 WebLAV installations Pro. You create the account by signing in with Google or with a link sent to your email. It is separate from WebLAV users. The account page is at `https://weblav.amiiby.com/account/`.

## Subscribe and link this WebLAV

1. In the admin pages, open the “Site settings” tab and press “Link to account” in the “Pro” section.

   ![The Pro section in Site settings](images/settings-pro.webp)

2. Scan the QR code with a smartphone, or press “Open the Pro service”.

   ![The page for linking to an account](images/pro-link.webp)

3. Sign in on the Pro service.
4. If you don't have Pro yet, press “Agree and subscribe monthly” or “Agree and subscribe yearly”, and pay on the payment page.
5. Press “Link this WebLAV”.

If the computer is connected to the internet, the WebLAV page switches to Pro within a few seconds.

> Press “Link this WebLAV” only when you opened the page from your own WebLAV. If you press it on a link someone else sent you, their WebLAV uses your Pro.

### Computers not connected to the internet

After linking, the Pro service page and an email show a 15-character code. Enter it in “Code” on the WebLAV page and press “Link with the code”.

Pro linked with a code has an end date. When it gets close, a notice appears in the “Pro” section. Get a new code with “Link again”. The end date is 7 days after the end of the period you have paid for, so the notice appears every month with a monthly subscription and every year with a yearly one.

## While you have Pro

A computer connected to the internet checks with the Pro service from time to time and extends Pro by itself. You don't need to do anything.

WebLAV sends the Pro service only a value that identifies the link, the site name, and the PC's name. The PC's name helps you tell your linked PCs apart on your account page. If you don't want to send that name, change your PC's name in the OS settings. It does not send files or information about users.

After you subscribe again or remove another computer, press “Check now” to apply the change right away.

## Remove

When you give the computer to someone else, or free a slot to link another computer, press “Remove from the account...” in the “Pro” section of that computer's WebLAV, then press “Remove”.

- The computer goes back to Free. Nothing over the limits is deleted, and you can still view, edit and delete it. You just can't add more.
- If WebLAV can't tell the Pro service about the removal, a QR code appears. Scan it with a smartphone and press “Free the slot” to free the slot.

If the computer is broken and you can't open its WebLAV page, press “Remove” under “Linked WebLAV installations” on the account page. The slot stays counted until the end date of the Pro that computer last received.

To move to a new computer, remove the old one first, then link the new one. Backups don't include the link to the account, so link again on a computer restored from a backup as well.

## Cancel

1. Open the account page. You can also open it with “Open the account page” in the “Pro” section of WebLAV.
2. Press “Manage billing (cancel, payment method, receipts)”, and cancel on the page that opens.

After you cancel, Pro stays until the end of the period you have paid for. When the period ends, the linked WebLAV installations go back to Free within about 7 days. Paid periods are not refunded for personal reasons. We refund the amount if we charged you twice, made an error in processing the payment, or Pro was not added after your payment went through. If you subscribed from outside Japan, you may get a refund under Link's refund policy within 60 days of purchase.

## When someone else takes over

With “Move Pro to another account” on the account page, you can move Pro and the linked WebLAV installations together to the account of another email address. You don't need to link the computers again. The billing contact and payment method don't move, so the new owner updates them with “Manage billing (cancel, payment method, receipts)” on the account page.
